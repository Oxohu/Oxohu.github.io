Nafisat Ozohu Abdulsalam

Federal University of Technology, Minna Mechatronics Engineering

I am an undergraduate student at the Department of Mechatronics Engineering (DOME) at the Federal University of Technology, Minna, Nigeria. Currently, I am contributing to medical imaging and digital health research and development as an intern at the Medical Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (MAI LAB) under the guidance of Dr Maruf and Dr Udunna. My research interests include Artificial Intelligence in its application to healthcare, specifically medical image analysis, Surgical Robotics Intelligence and Medical Devices.

What I Work On

Research Interests

Six threads that run through most of what I build and study.

MI

Medical Imaging

Segmentation and analysis of ultrasound and PET/CT scans to support faster, more reliable diagnosis.

AI

Medical AI

Deep learning architectures — U-Net, Attention U-Net, and beyond — applied to real clinical image data.

MD

Medical Devices

Designing and prototyping hardware for diagnostics, monitoring, and point-of-care use.

LR

AI for Low-Resource Healthcare

Building tools that stay useful where compute, connectivity, and specialists are scarce.

ES

Embedded Systems

Sensor networks and microcontroller-driven systems that bridge the physical and digital.

RB

Robotics

LLM-guided manipulation and verification pipelines aimed at safer autonomous action.
